feat(reader): replace the read-along narration bar with a pill and full player

Upstream's media-overlay mini player is a 480px bar across the bottom of the
reader that covers the text. Replace it with a small floating pill that shows
only with the reader toolbars, or on its own when the reader has moved away
from the narrated sentence (with "Back to narration" and "Narrate from here"
chips at its sides) or playback failed. The pill expands into a full player
with the audiobook cover, title, author, a chapter list limited to narrated
chapters, previous/next sentence, and speed and sleep menus. The sleep menu
adds an end-of-chapter option, and a running timer survives chapter jumps and
"Narrate from here" restarts. A "Listen with narration" chip above the footer
starts narration while the toolbars are showing.

Upstream's MediaOverlayMiniPlayer.vue and useMediaOverlay.ts stay untouched so
upstream merges apply cleanly; the hooks live in App.vue, ReaderView.vue and
useFoliate.ts.
