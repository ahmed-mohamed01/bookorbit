feat(dashboard): stack linked editions in Currently Reading

An ebook, its audiobook and a generated read-along are separate book records
joined by an edition link, so the Currently Reading widget listed a linked
work once per edition: three rows of the same title at the same percentage.
Fold the editions of one link into a single row whose cover is a deck in a
single cover's space, the lead in front, the other editions stepping out
behind it and a link badge pinned to the stack's corner, and make that row act for the edition the reader most recently spent
time with: clicking it opens that edition's detail page and Continue reading
resumes it, so switching from the ebook to the audiobook in Audiobookshelf
makes the audiobook the entry, and switching to the read-along makes the
read-along the entry.

Recency is the end of the latest recorded reading session, never a progress
row: the alignment sync projects audio position onto the ebook row, the
ebook-to-audio projection stamps the audiobook row, and the Audiobookshelf
sync writes the read-along row, while a session only comes from a reader, a
player or ingested Audiobookshelf listening. The dashboard widget service
asks a new optional CURRENTLY_READING_GROUP_SOURCE seam, same shape as
EXTRA_PROGRESS_SOURCE and provided by the edition-link module, which books
share a link and when each was last active, and stamps editionGroupId and
lastActivityAt onto those books; a failing source logs and leaves the list
flat. The client groups by that id, leads with the latest session and falls
back to the server's progress order on ties. Standalone rows are unchanged.
